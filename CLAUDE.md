# lockermate-support

Static support + privacy pages for the Locker Mate iPhone app, served by GitHub
Pages. The app itself lives in `~/Projects/LockerMate`.

**The privacy policy is a legal document that Apple checks against the App
Privacy answers in App Store Connect.** Do not edit `privacy.html` to say
something the app does not do, and do not let it go stale: if the app gains an
SDK, starts making network requests, or changes what it stores, update this page
in the same session.
